🔒 Privacy Policy

Air Drop - Android File Server

Effective date: September 2026

✅ No Personal Data Collected by the Developer

Air Drop - Android File Server ("the App") is developed by John Irianto Susanto. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

The Developer does not collect, store, or have access to your personal files, camera preview, or photos captured through the Remote Camera feature. File transfers, audio conversions, and Remote Camera operations are performed by the App on the user's device and through the App's local web server.

1. Information The Developer Does NOT Collect

The developer does not collect or have access to:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Browsing history
  • The actual contents of files transferred or converted
  • Camera preview data from the Remote Camera feature
  • Photos captured using the Remote Camera feature

Note on Device Identifiers: While the Developer does not directly use these identifiers for personal identification, third-party services used by the App, such as Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics, may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and usage information according to their respective policies.

2. How the File Server Works

The App creates a local Wi-Fi web server on your Android device. Files are transferred directly between your device and other devices connected to the same local network. No file data is routed through the Developer's server.

📶 File transfers are designed for local network use — the Developer does not receive or store the files being transferred.

3. Remote Camera

The App includes an optional Remote Camera feature that allows the user to use the Android device camera and control the camera through the App's web interface.

The camera is activated only when the user explicitly starts the Remote Camera feature. The App does not automatically activate the camera without the user's action to start the feature.

When Remote Camera is active:

  • The Android device camera provides a live camera preview through the App's web interface.
  • The user can remotely trigger the camera shutter from the web interface.
  • The camera/server activation is indicated to the user.
  • A sound effect is played when the Remote Camera server is started.
  • A shutter sound is played when a photo is captured.
  • The user can stop the Remote Camera feature at any time.
📷 Remote Camera is an optional user-initiated feature. The camera is not activated automatically by the App.

The Remote Camera feature uses the App's web server to provide camera controls and camera preview to a connected web browser. Camera preview data and captured photos are handled by the App and the connected devices and are not collected or stored by the Developer.

The Developer does not have access to the camera preview, captured photos, or Remote Camera sessions.

4. Audio Conversion

The App includes an Audio Converter (MP3 and OGG). This feature uses open-source libraries (LAME and AndroidX Media3) to convert files. All conversion happens on your device. The files you convert are not uploaded to or stored by the Developer.

These services are provided by Google and may collect information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and app usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable settings.
