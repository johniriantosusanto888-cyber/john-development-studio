🔒 Privacy Policy

Air Drop - Android File Server

Effective date: May 2026

✅ No Personal Data Collected by the Developer

Air Drop - Android File Server ("the App") is developed by John Irianto Susanto. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

The Developer does not collect, store, or have access to any personal data. All file transfers and audio conversions happen locally on your device.

1. Information The Developer Does NOT Collect

The developer does not collect or have access to:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Browsing history
  • The actual contents of files transferred or converted

Note on Device Identifiers: While the Developer does not use them, third-party services (see Section 4) may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads and track app usage.

2. How the File Server Works

The App creates a local Wi-Fi web server on your Android device. Files are transferred directly between your device and other devices on the same local network. No data is routed through the internet or any third-party server.

📶 All transfers are local network only — nothing leaves your network.

3. Audio Conversion

The App includes an Audio Converter (MP3 and OGG). This feature uses open-source libraries (LAME and AndroidX Media3) to convert files. All conversion happens 100% on your device. The files you convert are never uploaded or transmitted to the developer or any external server.

4. Advertising & Analytics

The App uses Google AdMob for advertising and Firebase Analytics for aggregated, anonymous usage statistics. These services are provided by Google and may collect Device IDs, Advertising IDs, IP addresses, and app usage data in accordance with their own privacy policy.

https://policies.google.com/privacy

This data collection is managed entirely by Google and is subject to Google's terms. The developer does not have access to any ad-related personal data.

5. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Third-Party Services & Open Source Licenses

The App uses the following third-party services and libraries:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements.
  • Firebase Analytics — for anonymous usage analytics.
  • LAME — Licensed under LGPL-2.1. Used for MP3 encoding.
  • AndroidX Media3 — Licensed under Apache 2.0. Used for OGG/Opus conversion.
