Delete Your BrewMaster Account & Data

Option 1 — Delete via the App

  1. Open BrewMaster
  2. Go to the Sync tab
  3. Tap Sign Out
  4. All your cloud sync data will be deleted from our servers

Option 2 — Revoke Google Access

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions
  2. Find BrewMaster in the list
  3. Tap Remove Access

Option 3 — Contact Us

Email: johniriantosusanto@gmail.com
Subject: "BrewMaster Data Deletion Request"
We will manually remove your data from Firebase within 7 business days.

Note: BrewMaster does not operate its own servers. Your sync data is stored in Google Firebase under your Google account. Local data on your device is removed automatically when you uninstall the app.
