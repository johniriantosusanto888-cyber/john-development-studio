BrewMaster is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not sell your personal data. Your custom recipes and grinder settings are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to sync them using your Google Account.

Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained on your device and is not collected by us in any way.

The app does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Sign-In (for Cloud Sync)

Cloud Sync & Google Sign-In

BrewMaster offers a Cloud Sync feature to back up your recipes and grinder settings across multiple devices.

  • Authentication: If you choose to use Cloud Sync, you will be asked to sign in with your Google Account.
  • Data Stored: We only store the data necessary for the app to function, which includes your custom recipes, grinder settings, and education progress (XP).
  • Storage: This data is securely stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and is linked directly to your Google Account credentials. We do not have access to your Google password.

Voice Guidance & Timer

The app includes a Guided Brew Timer with voice-guided instructions. This feature uses your device's native Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine. The app does not record your voice, nor does it require microphone permissions to function.

Smart Coffee Assistant

Our Smart Coffee Assistant provides recommendations based on the taste problems you select (e.g., "Too Bitter"). This feature processes the options you select to generate actionable advice. We do not collect personal data or track your specific coffee preferences.

Log Data

This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.
