Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained on your device and is not collected by us in any way.

File Server & Media Conversion

Our app allows you to transfer files over a local Wi-Fi network and convert video/audio files to other formats (e.g., MP3/OGG). These processes occur entirely on your device and within your local network. We do not have access to, nor do we store, any of the files you transfer or convert.

Remote Camera Feature

The Android File Server includes a "Remote Camera" feature that allows you to view your device's camera live and record video/audio from another device (like a web browser) on the same local network.

  • Camera & Microphone Permissions: The app requires access to your device's camera and microphone to function.
  • Data Streaming: The video and audio feeds are streamed directly to your local network client (browser). We do not route this data through any external servers or store it in the cloud.
  • Recordings: Any photos or videos recorded via the remote interface are saved locally on your device's storage. You have full control over these files.

This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.
