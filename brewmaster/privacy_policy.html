Privacy Policy — BrewMaster

Last updated: May 2026

BrewMaster ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Google Account — only if you choose to sign in for cloud sync. We store your email address solely to identify your sync data.
  • Recipe Data — your brew recipes and grinder settings, stored locally on your device and optionally synced to Firebase under your account.
  • No analytics or tracking — we do not collect usage data, crash reports, or advertising identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To sync your recipes across devices when you enable cloud sync.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your data to any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — used for cloud sync storage. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your recipe data is stored locally using Room database on your device. Cloud sync data is stored in Firebase Firestore, protected by Google's security infrastructure. We do not have access to your device's local data.

5. Children's Privacy

BrewMaster is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

You can delete your cloud data at any time by signing out and using the delete option in the Sync screen. Local data can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
