How to Join Closed Testing

Become a Beta Tester

Some of our apps are in closed testing and not yet publicly available on the Play Store. To get early access, you need to join our Google Group first — then install the test version directly from Google Play.

Step 1 — Join the Group
Go to our Google Group using your Gmail account and request to join:

john-development-studio-testers
Step 2 — Wait for Approval
Click Ask to join group on the Google Group page. We review requests and approve within 24 hours.
Step 3 — Open the Test Link
Once approved, click the Join Testing button on the app you want. You'll be redirected to Google Play to opt in as a tester.
Step 4 — Install & Give Feedback
After opting in on Google Play, the app will appear in your library. Install it and share your feedback — we read every review!
Use the same Gmail account for the Google Group and Google Play. It may take up to 1 hour after joining before the test link becomes active on your device.
