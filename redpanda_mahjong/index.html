🀄 Mahjong Fan Calculator

Playing with physical tiles? Input the tiles in your hand to instantly see your winning hand's fan score.

💡 Enter tiles separated by spaces. Use: 1m–9m (man/characters), 1p–9p (pin/circles), 1s–9s (sou/bamboo), E S W N (winds), C F P (dragons: red/white/green), Z (any honor).
Example: 1m 2m 3m 4p 5p 6p 7s 8s 9s E E → ready hand (13 tiles). Add one more tile to complete 14.

🎯 How to Play

Get started in minutes — from install to your first win.

1

Install the Game

Download from Google Play, or join as a closed tester via Google Group.

2

Choose Your Mode

Play Offline vs 3 AI bots, or Online multiplayer with friends using a 6-letter room code.

3

Know the Goal

Build 4 sets + 1 pair from 14 tiles. Sets = Pong (3 identical) or Chow (3 consecutive).

4

Win & Climb

Win by Tsumo (self-draw) or Ron (discard). Score points, climb the global leaderboard!
