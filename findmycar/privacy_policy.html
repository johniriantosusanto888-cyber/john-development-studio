Privacy Policy

Find My Car

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Thank you for using Find My Car. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Find My Car may access the following information:

  • Location – Used to save your parking location and navigate you back to your vehicle.
  • Notifications – Used to remind you before your parking time expires and when the parking timer has ended.
  • Alarms & Reminders – Used to deliver accurate parking timer alerts even when the app is not open.
  • Storage Access – Used only when you choose to export or import your parking history as a CSV backup file.

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, messages, or any other personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Save your parking location.
  • Store optional parking notes such as floor number, parking zone, or nearby landmarks.
  • Launch Google Maps navigation to your saved parking location.
  • Display parking countdown notifications.
  • Remind you before your parking time expires.
  • Export and import your parking history as CSV backup files.

3. Data Storage

Your parking information is stored locally on your device.

This includes:

  • Parking coordinates
  • Parking notes
  • Parking time limit
  • Parking history
  • CSV backup files (only if you choose to export them)

We do not upload or store your parking information on our servers.

4. Export & Import

Find My Car allows you to export your parking history as a CSV file and import it later for backup or transferring data to another device.

These files remain under your control and are stored only on your device or in the location you choose.

5. Third-Party Services

The application uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Maps
  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Advertising

If ads are displayed, Google AdMob may collect limited information such as your advertising identifier to provide relevant advertisements.

Find My Car does not receive or store this advertising information.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the app may request:

  • Precise Location
  • Notifications
  • Alarms & Reminders
  • Storage Access (for Export & Import Backup)

Each permission is used only for its intended purpose and is never used to collect personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Find My Car is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published within the application or on the Privacy Policy webpage.
