Privacy Policy

Virtual Pet — Mobile Game

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Welcome to Virtual Pet ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile game ("Virtual Pet" or "the App"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Quick Summary: Virtual Pet is a casual offline-friendly pet simulation game. We collect minimal data needed to run the game, serve ads, and provide optional online features like the global leaderboard. We never sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Display Name — Optional. You may choose a nickname when submitting your score to the global leaderboard.
  • Avatar — Optional. An emoji you select as your profile icon for the leaderboard.
  • Pet Names — Names you give to your virtual pets. Stored locally on your device.

1.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information — Device model, operating system version, language, and country.
  • Game Progress — Your pet levels, coins, diamonds, achievements, and mini-game scores. Stored locally on your device.
  • Anonymous User ID — A randomly generated ID (via Firebase Anonymous Authentication) for leaderboard submissions.
  • Analytics Data — Anonymous usage statistics (game sessions, screen views, feature usage) via Firebase Analytics.
  • Advertising ID — A resettable identifier used by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Real name (unless you type it yourself in display name)
  • ❌ Email address or phone number
  • ❌ Precise location (GPS)
  • ❌ Photos, contacts, or files on your device
  • ❌ Microphone or camera access
  • ❌ Payment information (no in-app purchases currently)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

Purpose Data Used
Run the game and save your progress Game progress, pet names
Display the global leaderboard Display name, avatar, high score, anonymous ID
Serve advertisements Advertising ID, device info
Improve the game & fix bugs Analytics data, device info
Prevent cheating & abuse Anonymous ID, leaderboard data

3. Third-Party Services

Virtual Pet uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

3.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID and device information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

3.2 Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the game. All data collected is anonymous and aggregated.

Firebase Privacy →

3.3 Firebase Authentication

We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to generate a unique ID for leaderboard submissions. No personal information is required.

3.4 Firebase Firestore

We use Firebase Firestore to store leaderboard data (display name, avatar, high score, anonymous ID). This data is publicly visible on the global leaderboard.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage — Game progress is saved locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.
  • Backup Files — Optional automatic backups are stored in your device's app-private storage. You may also export/import backup files manually.
  • Cloud Storage — Only leaderboard data (display name, avatar, high score) is stored on Firebase servers.
  • Security — We use industry-standard security practices. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
⚠️ Important: Your game progress is stored locally. If you uninstall the app or lose your device without a backup, your progress may be lost permanently. We recommend using the in-app backup feature regularly.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with:

  • Google AdMob — For advertising purposes
  • Firebase — For analytics, authentication, and leaderboard
  • Legal Authorities — If required by law

6. Children's Privacy

Virtual Pet is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Rights

7.1 All Users

  • Reset Advertising ID — Via Android Settings → Google → Ads
  • Opt out of personalized ads — Via same menu
  • Delete local data — Uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Delete leaderboard entry — Contact us at the email below

7.2 EU/UK Users (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/UK, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

7.3 California Users (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under CCPA, including the right to know what data is collected and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

8. Data Retention

  • Local game data — Stored as long as the app is installed
  • Leaderboard data — Retained while your account is active
  • Analytics data — Retained per Firebase policy (up to 14 months)

9. International Data Transfers

Our third-party services (Google, Firebase) may transfer data to servers located in various countries, including the United States. These transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.
